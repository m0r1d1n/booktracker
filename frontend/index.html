Paste one ISBN per line (ISBN-10 or ISBN-13). Metadata and cover art are fetched automatically from Open Library / Google Books.

Upload a CSV/TSV export from your previous tracker (Title, Author, ISBN, Publisher, Date Read, Shelves, tags, owned, dropped_dates, currently_reading, etc. — columns are matched by name, extras are ignored). Rows whose ISBN already matches a book in your library will update that book's status, dates, rating, and tags instead of duplicating it.

Upload a CSV/TSV with a Location (or Room / Storage Location) column plus an ISBN or Title column, to bulk-tag books with where they live in storage. If you're re-uploading a copy of this app's own CSV export, its ID column (if present) is used for matching — the most reliable option. Otherwise matches by ISBN, then an unambiguous exact title match; any row where the Title column disagrees with the matched book is skipped and flagged rather than applied. Existing books only — this doesn't add new ones. Don't reuse your library export's Shelves/Bookshelves columns here — in that format those already mean reading status and genre tags, not physical location.
